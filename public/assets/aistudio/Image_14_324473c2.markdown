## Brand & Style
The design system is engineered to evoke a sense of **prestige, permanence, and judicial authority**. It serves an elite academic and legal alumni network, prioritizing high-fidelity craftsmanship over fleeting digital trends.

The visual style is a sophisticated blend of **Modern Corporate** and **Tactile Academic**. It utilizes high-contrast relationships between deep slates and parchment tones to mimic the experience of reading a formal legal brief. Key brand pillars include:
- **Legacy:** Through the use of timeless serif typography and "Muted Imperial Gold" accents.
- **Integrity:** Represented by rigid, structured layouts and a lack of decorative fluff.
- **Tradition:** Communicated via subtle textures and formal, high-contrast borders.

The "LEGACY | INTEGRITY | TRADITION" motto block should be treated as a ceremonial anchor, often placed within a gold-bordered container with a slight inner shadow to mimic a wax seal or a metal plaque.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in historical gravitas. 

- **Primary (Deep Navy Slate):** Used for structural headers, primary buttons, and critical iconography. It represents the "Bench" and the weight of law.
- **Secondary (Muted Imperial Gold):** Used sparingly for highlighting excellence, borders on premium components, and the "Legacy" motto bar.
- **Surface (Off-White Parchment):** The primary background color. It should never be pure #FFFFFF; the slight warmth of parchment reduces eye strain and reinforces the academic narrative.
- **Text (Charcoal):** Provides maximum legibility without the harshness of pure black, maintaining a "printed ink" feel.

Apply a subtle, low-opacity noise texture (2-3%) to the Parchment surfaces to simulate high-quality paper stock.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy relies on the tension between the authoritative **Libre Caslon Text** (Serif) and the pragmatic **Work Sans** (Sans-Serif).

- **Headlines:** Use Caslon for all titles. It provides the "Judicial" weight required for names of Justices and Chief Judges.
- **Body Text:** Work Sans is chosen for its neutrality and readability in dense lists of names or legal descriptions. 
- **Motto/Labels:** Use the `label-caps` style for the "Legacy | Integrity | Tradition" branding. The increased letter spacing is essential for maintaining the "official" look of the header block.

## Layout & Spacing
This design system utilizes a **Fixed, Centered Grid** for desktop to mimic the layout of a formal document or a letterhead. 

- **The Grid:** A 12-column system with generous 24px gutters. 
- **Margins:** Large horizontal margins on desktop (64px+) reinforce the feeling of a prestigious printed page.
- **Rhythm:** Use the 8px base unit. Component vertical spacing should be airy (`unit-lg`) to allow the high-contrast elements "room to breathe."
- **Reflow:** On mobile, the margins compress to 16px, and multi-column lists (like the "Bench" and "Bar") reflow into a single-column stack.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Structural Outlines**.

- **Primary Elevation:** Achieved through 1px solid borders in Muted Imperial Gold or a 15% opacity of Deep Navy Slate.
- **Layering:** Elements "lift" from the Parchment background using subtle "inset" shadows or thin gold strokes rather than external blurs.
- **Contrast:** Use high-contrast color blocks (Navy containers on Parchment backgrounds) to define hierarchy.
- **Gold Accents:** Use a very slight linear gradient on gold borders (Top-left to Bottom-right) to suggest a metallic sheen without becoming skeuomorphic.

## Shapes
To maintain a solemn and judicial atmosphere, the design system utilizes **Sharp (0px)** corners for all primary containers, buttons, and input fields. 

Rounded corners are perceived as friendly or casual; sharp corners suggest discipline, architecture, and legal formality. The only exception is the "Motto Bar," which may have a subtle `0.25rem` (Soft) radius to match the pill-like branding seen in historical letterheads.

## Components
- **Buttons:** Sharp corners, Deep Navy Slate background, Off-White Parchment text. Primary buttons should have a 1px Gold bottom-border.
- **The Motto Bar:** A specialized component. Gold background (#C5A059), Charcoal text, with a thin Navy stroke. Always centered.
- **Cards:** Parchment background with a 1px Navy Slate border. For "featured" alumni or justices, upgrade the border to Muted Imperial Gold.
- **Input Fields:** No background (transparent), with a 1px Charcoal bottom-border only. Labels use `label-caps`.
- **Dividers:** Use a "Triple Line" divider (thin-thick-thin) in Navy Slate to separate major sections like "The Bench" and "The Bar," referencing classical legal typesetting.
- **Lists:** Alumni lists should be left-aligned with significant leading (line-height) to ensure each name carries individual weight.
